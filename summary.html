The Map of My Journey

Beyond My Comfort Zone

Gianluca Valentini Wilfrid Laurier University BU301 · The Entrepreneurial Method Professor Laura Allan · Fall 2026
  1. 1

    Discovering My WHY

    The Island of Discovery

    What I thought
    I saw my experiences as separate challenges.
    What I learned
    They were connected by the same desire to explore, grow, take action, and connect with people.
  2. 2

    Turning Frustration into Opportunity

    The Factory of Possibilities

    What I thought
    My different internship experiences were part of my personal learning.
    What I learned
    They could also help me recognize a problem worth solving for other people.
  3. 3

    Learning to Take Small Risks

    The Bridge of Courage

    What I thought
    I needed a clear plan and enough resources to pursue the complete idea.
    What I learned
    I can take a small step, learn from it, and decide what to do next.
  4. 4

    Learning to Listen

    The Crossroads of Perspectives

    What I thought
    If I had a promising idea, I should develop and perfect it.
    What I learned
    An idea can become stronger when I allow other people to challenge it.
  5. 5

    Becoming a Different Thinker

    The Horizon of Growth

    What I thought
    Progress meant choosing a goal and following a clear route toward it.
    What I learned
    Progress can also mean adapting the route as I learn, without losing sight of what matters to me.

The map is complete. My journey isn’t.

This is not the end. It’s my starting point.
